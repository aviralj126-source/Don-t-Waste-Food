Think Before You Scrape Your Plate! 🍽️

A student-led project exploring why good food gets thrown away, how it affects our planet, and practical things we can do every day.

📝 Why I Chose This Project

Hi! I decided to build this website after a school event last month. Walking past the cafeteria trash bins, I saw entire untouched sandwiches, barely eaten apples, and half-full rice bowls being dumped straight into garbage bags. Then last weekend at a cousin's wedding, more than five large catering trays of delicious curry and bread were thrown out at midnight.

It made me really uncomfortable. In our environmental science classes, we talk so much about saving electricity and plastic pollution, but food waste rarely gets the same attention. So for this project, I researched the real facts and practical solutions. I hope this website gives you a few ideas you can try at home!
