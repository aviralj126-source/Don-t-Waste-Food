📊 The Real Numbers

The Real Impact on Planet, People & Money 🌍

When we throw food in the trash, we aren't just wasting an apple or a crust of bread. We are wasting the water, land, labor, and fuel that went into producing it.

1.05 Billion
Tonnes of Food Wasted

Wasted globally across households, restaurants, and retail stores every single year — about 19% of all food available to consumers.

Source: UNEP Food Waste Index Report (2024)
8% – 10%
Of Global GHG Emissions

Food loss and waste is responsible for up to 10% of total world greenhouse gas emissions — nearly 5 times more than the aviation sector.

Source: UN Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)
783 Million
People Face Chronic Hunger

While over 1 billion meals are thrown away every single day, nearly 783 million people do not have enough food to eat.

Source: UN FAO & World Food Programme (SOFI Report)
$1 Trillion
Economic Loss Each Year

The direct global economic cost of wasted agricultural food is approximately 1 trillion US dollars every year.

Source: Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO)

📖 Student Reflection: What Surprised Me Most

Before this project, I always thought: "It's just food, it will rot and become soil anyway, so what's the big deal?" But I learned in science class that when food is dumped into landfills, it gets crushed under mountains of other trash without oxygen. Instead of turning into healthy compost, it produces Methane (CH4), which is over 25 times more potent than carbon dioxide at warming our atmosphere!

Three Ways Food Waste Affects Us

🌱

1. Environmental Cost

Agriculture uses huge amounts of fresh water, arable land, and energy. Producing a single hamburger takes around 2,400 litres of water! When that burger gets thrown into the trash, all that clean water and fuel is wasted forever.

🌍 Landfills full of food waste are major methane hotspots.
💸

2. Financial Cost

The average household throws away hundreds of dollars worth of uneaten groceries every year. That is literally like buying fresh vegetables and immediately dropping currency notes straight into the garbage bin!

💰 Saving food means saving household grocery bills.
🤝

3. The Human & Ethical Cost

There is more than enough food produced on Earth to feed every single man, woman, and child. Food waste is not a shortage problem — it is an ethical and distribution failure when edible meals are discarded near hungry communities.

🍲 1 billion meals are wasted every day worldwide.
