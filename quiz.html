The Food Saver Quiz 🧠

Test what you've learned! Answer these 5 multiple-choice questions to see your final score and read the explanations.

✏️ How It Works

Pick one answer for each question below, then click "Submit My Answers". Your score will be calculated instantly and correct answers will be highlighted in green with explanations from my research!

1. Approximately how much edible food is wasted globally every year?

💡 Explanation: According to the UNEP Food Waste Index Report (2024), about 1.05 billion tonnes of food is wasted annually — nearly 19% of all food available to consumers.

2. What is the real difference between "Best Before" and "Use By" dates?

💡 Explanation: "Best Before" guarantees peak flavor and quality (safe to eat afterwards). "Use By" is the safety cut-off, especially for meat and dairy.

3. What powerful greenhouse gas is produced when food rots in an airtight landfill?

💡 Explanation: Under oxygen-free conditions in landfills, anaerobic bacteria produce Methane (CH4), which is over 25 times more potent than CO2 at trapping atmospheric heat!

4. What does the kitchen storage rule "FIFO" stand for?

💡 Explanation: "First In, First Out" means placing newly bought groceries behind older ones so the older food gets consumed before spoiling.

5. Where does the largest share of consumer food waste actually happen?

💡 Explanation: UNEP found that ~60% of all food wasted at consumer level happens in private household kitchens — meaning our daily choices matter the most!
